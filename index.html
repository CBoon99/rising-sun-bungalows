The untouched Gili

Meno is the first choice when you want the Gilis without the 18–25 pub crawls of Trawangan—or the full hippie / healer / life-coach circuit. Bicycles, beach time, real reefs, and room to breathe.

Turtles & shallow reefs

Turtle City and east-coast snorkelling deliver daily encounters with green and hawksbill turtles over gardens of hard coral.

Wall dives nearby

Meno Wall, Secret Reef, Bounty Wreck, and classic Gili sites sit minutes away by boat—turtles, macro, and seasonal surprises.

No traffic stress

Pedal the island loop in under two hours, hop a cidomo cart, or simply walk the shoreline—no scooters, no gridlock.

Beach walks & boats

Bikes & shade

Logistics

How to get there

We're about 2 minutes on foot from Gili Meno harbour. No cars or scooters on the Gilis—walk, bike, or cidomo once you land.

From Bali

Fast boat

About 1.5–2 hours from Sanur or Padang Bai area. Some boats call at Trawangan or Air first—hop a short inter-island boat to Meno if needed.

From Lombok

Bangsal boat

Public or private boat from Bangsal harbour (or nearby ports) — typically 20–40 minutes to Gili Meno harbour.

Airport LOP

Fly, road, boat

Lombok International Airport to Bangsal by road (about 1–1.5 hrs depending on traffic), then boat to Meno. Ask when booking for driver pointers.

Gili T or Air

Island hop

Short public or private boat between the three Gilis — usually 5–20 minutes. Easy if you're already in the archipelago.

Once you land on Meno

01Step off at Gili Meno harbour.
02About a 2-minute walk — message us your arrival boat and we'll meet you or send pin directions.
03Luggage? A cidomo (horse cart) can drop you at the gate for a small fee if you prefer not to walk.
